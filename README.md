# Hi, I'm Favour 👋

I work at the intersection of **computational solid mechanics, finite element analysis (FEA), and materials informatics** to investigate deformation, strain localization, and degradation mechanics in high-performance materials.

My work bridges physical metallurgy with modern computing: using numerical simulations and data-driven methods to evaluate structural behavior before conducting expensive laboratory synthesis.

---

### 🔧 What I Work On
* **Computational Solid Mechanics:** Elastic-plastic stress analysis, strain localization, and non-linear finite element modeling (ANSYS, SOLIDWORKS Simulation).
* **Materials Informatics & Software:** Architecting web platforms and relational data schemas to parse, query, and screen multivariate materials property datasets.
* **Complex Alloy Deformation:** Investigating dislocation pathways, local chemical ordering, and strength-ductility trade-offs in multi-principal element alloys.

---

### 🔬 Research Interests
* **Physics-Informed Machine Learning (PIML):** Embedding thermodynamic boundaries and constitutive equations into predictive models for corrosion and mechanical performance.
* **Continuum Mechanics & Plasticity:** Simulating localized stress concentrations, cold-working deformation, and fracture thresholds under extreme environments.
* **Degradation & Interfacial Mechanics:** Stress-corrosion cracking, hydrogen embrittlement, and passive film stability in clean energy infrastructure.
* **Circular Metallurgy & Metal Recovery:** Hydrometallurgical leaching kinetics and critical raw material upcycling.

---

### 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Simulation & CAD:** ANSYS Mechanical (APDL, Non-linear FEA), SOLIDWORKS, SOLIDWORKS Simulation
* **Scientific Computing & ML:** Python (NumPy, Pandas, Scikit-learn, Matplotlib), MATLAB, OriginPro
* **Backend & Web Data:** PostgreSQL / Supabase, SQL, Git & GitHub, Markdown, LaTeX
* **Characterization & Lab:** Optical Microscopy, Metallographic Preparation, Tensile & Hardness Testing

---

### 🚀 Projects & Active Repositories
* **[Susengine](https://susengine.lovable.app/):** An open-access computational materials platform coupling physical property databases with parametric screening algorithms for engineering selection.
* **FEA Cold-Working Stress Analysis:** Numerical simulation models and boundary formulations evaluating continuous stress distribution and plastic strain localization during metal cold forming (accompanying *SETJ*, 2026).
* **Materials Informatics & Data Pipelines:** Python scripts and numerical routines for stress-strain curve fitting, data scatter reduction, and materials regression models.
